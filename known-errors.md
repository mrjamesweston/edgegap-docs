# This is a basic list of issues you may encounter with very simple solutions.
  ?> Ran into something you couldn't find in this page? [DM AUSTIN ON TWITTER](https://twitter.com/austinjgaudet) or on Discord at austin.james.

  > Can't verify my Edgegap Token
  When you're entering your [Edgegap Token that you retrieved from your Dashboard](https://app.edgegap.com/user-settings?tab=tokens), ensure the token that you are getting the One-Click Token. This is denoted by it stating that under the Quick Start category by saying Active. Once the token is input into the Edgegap Plugin **ENSURE YOU ARE HITTING THE ENTER KEY ON YOUR KEYBOARD**. This pushes the token, the Verify button should light up a bit. When that happens, click the Verify button and it should let you know it's verified!

  ![edgegap dashboard](https://i.imgur.com/KCiU7z0.png)

  > Okay I was able to verify my token, but I'm unable to create a new app?
  Most times the error from this is when your selected App Image is one of the following: the image doesn't "exist" or is too large (whether it's image size or how much space it takes up).
 For the image not existing, go back and try to reselect it!
 For the image being too "large". I would recommend it be in 256x256 and not exceed 600kb just to be safe.
