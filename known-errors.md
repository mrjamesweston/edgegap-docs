  > Can't verify my Edgegap Token
  When you're entering your [Edgegap Token that you retrieved from your Dashboard](https://app.edgegap.com/user-settings?tab=tokens), ensure the token that you are getting the One-Click Token. This is denoted by it stating that under the Quick Start category by saying Active.
